500 New School Kits Delivered Ahead of Term

🎒

Our education team completed delivery of 500 complete school kits to five partner schools across three districts. Each kit includes notebooks, pens, uniforms, and shoes — everything a child needs to start the new academic year.

"Last year my children had to share one notebook between three of them," said Grace N., a parent at Mukono Primary. "This year, each of them has their own. It sounds small, but it changes everything."

The distribution was funded by individual donors and our corporate partner TechCorp Foundation. Total cost: $12,500 — less than $25 per child.
