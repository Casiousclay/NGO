About Casious Foundation

Community-led change since 2014.

Our Story

Casious Foundation began when a group of teachers, nurses, and community organizers came together to address gaps in education and healthcare that governments couldn't reach. Today we operate in 18 communities with a team of 45 staff and 200+ volunteers.

Mission, Vision & Values

Mission: Empower underserved communities through sustainable programs in education, health, and livelihoods.

Vision: Thriving communities where every person has access to opportunity.

Values: Transparency · Community ownership · Measurable impact · Dignity for all

Financial Transparency

94% of funds go directly to programs. 4% administration, 2% fundraising. Full audited financials available in our annual report.

Leadership

Our board includes community representatives, development experts, and finance professionals. Team photos and bios will be added with your real content.
