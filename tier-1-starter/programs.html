Our Programs

Three pillars of change in the communities we serve.

📚

Education for All

Scholarships, school supplies, and tutoring for 2,400+ children. We partner with local schools to keep kids in class.

🏥

Community Health

Mobile clinics visit remote villages monthly. Maternal care, vaccinations, and health education for families.

💼

Skills & Livelihoods

Vocational training in tailoring, farming, and small business. Micro-grants help graduates launch their own ventures.
